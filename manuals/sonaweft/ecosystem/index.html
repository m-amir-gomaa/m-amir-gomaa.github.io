Usage / Sonaweft

From sources to connected notes

Move reviewed Pageweft and Sonaweft exports into Noteweld with explicit validation and separate account boundaries.

12 November 20262 min readDownload Markdown ↓
Current public preview supports verified email-first signup, mailed resets for verified addresses and existing private-key recovery. Processing may be temporarily unavailable; completed examples remain open. Google/GitHub sign-in and owner account linking have passed their recorded acceptance. Paid checkout is closed. Dated release records describe their own checkpoints. Reviewed source revision 825f4b5 is reserved for owner-authorized review; a source checkpoint does not establish deployment. This public edition is readable independently; public source samples have their own explicit licenses.

Pageweft converts PDF pages to Markdown. Sonaweft transcribes recordings. Noteweld keeps editable notes, links, source material and portable vaults.

  1. Use Pageweft or Sonaweft, review the original against the result, and save any corrections.
  2. Download a fresh complete ZIP bundle from that application’s workspace.
  3. In Noteweld, open Source bundles, choose Pageweft or Sonaweft export, and validate the ZIP.
  4. Review its contents, confirm permission to retain them, and import. Notes and their original archive commit together. The original extraction, corrections and optional AI notes keep separate labels.
  5. Edit and link the imported notes. Download the original bundle or export a portable vault when needed.

The current Noteweld preview accepts an archive up to 4 MiB and 250 notes, subject to remaining account capacity. Pageweft can export fewer pages and omit the source PDF. A smaller recording gives a smaller Sonaweft bundle. Keep your own originals; a bundle includes source media only when the exporter includes it.

Accounts, recovery, storage limits, balances and payment preparation belong to each app separately. Opening a link transfers no private files. There is no shared sign-in, automatic cross-app copy or combined subscription. Processing credits and Noteweld managed storage are different services. Live checkout remains disabled while merchant/provider acceptance is unfinished.

Private original files are never put in a link or query string. There is no background upload when visiting another app. Import validates paths, size, declared hashes and references before saving. Hashes prove consistency, not OCR/transcription accuracy. Verify important passages against the source.
