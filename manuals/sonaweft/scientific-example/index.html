Listen to a real scientific explanation

The completed Sonaweft example is a continuous 63.65-second NOAA Ocean Podcast excerpt about why gravity varies across Earth. Megan Forbes interviews physicist Derek van Westrum in Understanding Gravity, episode 33 . The selected passage runs from 181.05 to 244.70 seconds in the source episode.

The example credits NOAA and the speakers, links the original source and states that no endorsement is implied. It is third-party source material, not an original recording made for this portfolio. The excerpt contains speech rather than a music insert; the reuse review is specific to this selected source.

What you are seeing

The saved transcript comes from actual Whisper recognition. Recognition, manual corrections, source attribution and editorial listening notes remain separate. The listening notes are explicitly labeled as prepared with AI assistance; they are not output from Sonaweft’s optional review provider. No new processing job is needed to open the example.

Listen, follow timestamps, try the review controls and download the complete bundle. Source offsets and hashes preserve traceability; a good short example does not certify transcription accuracy on every recording.
