An ordered readiness checklist; owner-specific account questions are omitted from this public edition.

Current public preview supports email/passphrase and private-key recovery. Free processing may be temporarily unavailable; completed examples remain open. Google and mailed email reset remain unconfigured for this product. Paid billing is closed; earlier release records describe dated milestones. Production source revision b718402 is reserved for owner-authorized review. This reviewed public edition is readable independently; public source samples have their own explicit licenses. This is an explicitly reviewed public summary; private operational and learner details are omitted.

What visitors can use

The completed PDF example, public guide, private email account/recovery flow and bounded processing interface are deployed. Live sample availability is checked privately and may temporarily close; an example remains useful without another processing job.

What is still gated

Paid processing packs remain closed. App-domain approval is owner-reported; actual seller/payout readiness and genuine payment/refund/payout acceptance still require their own evidence. Do not infer merchant readiness from a successful processing demo or mocked payment tests.

Current scope, retention, extraction routes and review controls are in Using Pageweft . Source inspection and error flags do not certify OCR fidelity. A complete synthetic text-only book is not a representative difficult-book OCR benchmark.

Owner controls stay private

Guest accounting amounts, provider usage, reset details and private reports are not part of the visitor contract. Availability can close when eligibility or safety evidence is unavailable. Customer processing quotes remain a separate contract.

Provider boundaries distinguish deployed product functions from remaining commercial acceptance.
