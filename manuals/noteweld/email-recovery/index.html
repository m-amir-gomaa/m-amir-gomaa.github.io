Email signup and password recovery

Verified signup, one-use inbox links, password resets and legacy recovery.

Create an email account

Open signup , complete the security check and request your email. Follow the link, choose your name and passphrase, then confirm. Opening the link alone creates no account. Links expire after 30 minutes and work once. Use at least 15 characters, following the form’s full password requirements.

Recover access

Verified email/password accounts can request a reset . Completing the emailed form changes the password and signs out previous sessions. Any old private recovery key stops working. Unverified legacy accounts retain their existing key/provider methods; inbox ownership never silently merges or takes over an older account.

What was checked

The owner completed actual security widgets in Firefox. Signup and reset messages reached the inbox; explicit confirmation, reused-link refusal, old-session/password rejection, replacement login and temporary-account deletion passed. Local suites cover additional expiry, concurrency and provider-failure boundaries. Separate raw widget-token replay was not captured. Google/GitHub consent remains a separate acceptance task.

Using Noteweld covers the normal workspace. Private anti-abuse budgets and provider credentials are not published.
