## Source and interpretation stay separate

Sonaweft accepts authorized English recordings, produces timestamped speech recognition, and keeps original text separate from later corrections, speaker-label annotations, optional AI notes and external research candidates. Manual labels do not establish speaker identity; recognition and AI output require review against the recording.

Inspection and quotes precede processing. The bounded sample is subject to private availability. Optional transcript review sends text to the named provider only after consent; audio is not sent to that text provider. Selected-span jobs retain original recording offsets and do not erase older work. Explicit retries use fresh quotes and retained recognition where available.

## Retain useful work

Listen to source segments, save separate corrections with revision checks, compare/restore retained annotations, and export Markdown/text/JSON/subtitles or a hashed source-linked bundle. Noteweld's current importer can preview and atomically import its supported bundle within its own limits. The two applications do not share login, balances or storage.

Hosted files normally expire after 48 hours. Immediate deletion closes access while financial records remain separate. Processing packs are configured, but paid checkout stays disabled pending genuine commercial/provider acceptance. See [current tariffs](/manuals/sonaweft/overview/) and [the practical guide](/manuals/sonaweft/guide/).

## Quality boundary

An attractive short example and passing transport/account tests do not prove accuracy on every recording, representative long-recording margins, certified transcription, verified speakers or user demand. Local CLI setup, historical agent concepts and hosted processing are distinct workflows. Private owner planning and working-copy product edits are not republished as a current public contract.
