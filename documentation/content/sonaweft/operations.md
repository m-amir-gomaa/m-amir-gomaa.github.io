## Current public operating boundary

Sonaweft runs behind its public Cloudflare gateway on Modal. Email-first signup, verification and mailed resets for verified addresses are deployed alongside private-key recovery. Accounts and balances are product-specific. Google/GitHub owner sign-in/linking acceptance has passed; checkout is closed.

## Admission and privacy

Live free processing checks visitor eligibility and private shared availability before dispatch. An unavailable or unverifiable admission check refuses new processing with **Currently unavailable. Please try again later.** Completed examples remain open. Signing in does not reset sample eligibility.

Owner accounting amounts, spending settings, usage, reset details and private reports are deliberately omitted from public documentation and responses. Customer quotes and balances are a separate contract. An admission estimate is not proof of actual provider charges or a physically isolated provider billing account.

## Durable work and recovery

The application records processing state and accounting transactionally, separates source/derived material, and checks ownership before private downloads. Retries and partial failures have explicit state and settlement behavior. Download retained outputs before their normal 48-hour expiry; immediate deletion closes access and removes supported files while financial records remain separate.

Owner pause, diagnostics, backup and recovery commands are private operational tools. They are not public administration routes. Preserve source/ledger ownership, idempotency and admission gates during recovery; do not reset quotas or disable guards to reopen a trial.

## Payment and quality evidence

The owner reports domain approval; seller/payout readiness and genuine payment/refund/payout acceptance remain separate. No billing activation or provider account change was performed by this guide refresh. Representative OCR/transcription quality, scale and margins require their own evidence.
