Pageweft converts PDF pages to Markdown. Sonaweft transcribes recordings.
Noteweld keeps editable notes, links, source material and portable vaults.

1. Use Pageweft or Sonaweft, review the original against the result, and save any corrections.
2. Download a **fresh complete ZIP bundle** from that application's workspace.
3. In Noteweld, open **Source bundles**, choose **Pageweft or Sonaweft export**, and validate the ZIP.
4. Review its contents, confirm permission to retain them, and import. Notes and their original archive commit together. The original extraction, corrections and optional AI notes keep separate labels.
5. Edit and link the imported notes. Download the original bundle or export a portable vault when needed.

The current Noteweld preview accepts an archive up to **4 MiB** and 250 notes,
subject to remaining account capacity. Pageweft can export fewer pages and omit
the source PDF. A smaller recording gives a smaller Sonaweft bundle. Keep your
own originals; a bundle includes source media only when the exporter includes it.

Accounts, recovery, storage limits, balances and payment preparation belong to
each app separately. Opening a link transfers no private files. There is no
shared sign-in, automatic cross-app copy or combined subscription. Processing
credits and Noteweld managed storage are different services. Live checkout
remains disabled while merchant/provider acceptance is unfinished.

Private original files are never put in a link or query string. There is no
background upload when visiting another app. Import validates paths, size,
declared hashes and references before saving. Hashes prove consistency, not
OCR/transcription accuracy. Verify important passages against the source.
