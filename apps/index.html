BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Try freely.
Know what happens next.

What happens when I open an example?

No personal file is uploaded and no new transcription or conversion runs. You can explore the original demonstration content and download its files.

Where do example edits go?

They stay in your browser. Noteweld and Pageweft example drafts live in the current tab; download before leaving. Sonaweft's example can keep review edits in this browser. These are separate from private account workspaces.

Can I use my own files?

Yes, when the relevant hosted processing is available. The apps inspect your file and explain the next step before processing. Live free samples use shared limits and pause if hosting-budget safety cannot be verified. The completed examples remain available.

When should I create an account?

Use an account to return to your private workspace across browsers. Email and a passphrase are supported. Keep the private recovery key shown at sign-up; no email password-reset service is connected. Signing in does not reset free processing limits.

How long are hosted files kept?

Pageweft PDFs and Sonaweft recordings/results normally expire after 48 hours. Download what you want to keep. Noteweld account notes remain until you delete them, within its preview limits. Each app's guide explains its storage and privacy boundaries.
